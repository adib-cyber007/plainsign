## Facts code changes cannot resolve

These must be confirmed before representing the entry as complete:

- Participant age, residence, sanctions/conflicts and employer obligations (Rules 3).
- Every member's registration, one team/product per participant, and express rules acceptance (Rules 4, 6–7).
- Rights and permission for submitted code, graphics, names and likenesses; any sponsorship approval (Rules 9, 12, 17).
- Actual funding history: the FAQ restricts eligibility to new startups without significant outside funding and supplies no numeric threshold. Ask the organizer if the history is ambiguous.

Do not change names, dates, funding or development history to evade these conditions. An eligibility exclusion requires an organizer decision, not a specification change. No rule acceptance or registration was performed by this review.
